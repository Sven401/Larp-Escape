H1 - TS3USB221ARSER: guaranteed isolation during partial supply and ramps
Subject: TS3USB221ARSER - guaranteed leakage/isolation below 2.3 V VCC

Hello TI Support,

We are qualifying a USB data-isolation circuit using TS3USB221ARSER. We have
reviewed SCDS277C Rev C, including the VCC=0 Ioff and powered IOZ limits. We
would like to establish which guaranteed limits, if any, apply between these
specified operating states.

Conditions:
- Ambient temperature: -20 to +60 degC.
- VCC held at intermediate levels or ramping between 0 and 2.3 V, especially
  0.05, 0.2, 0.8, 1.5, 1.8 and 2.29 V.
- Data-pin voltages: 0 to 3.6 V; one side may remain powered while the other
  side and/or the switch supply are off.
- Common GND; host connected to D+/D-, target connected to 1D+/1D-;
  unused 2D+/2D- left unconnected. The VCC rail has a 10 kohm bleeder.
- S tied to GND. OE pulled to VCC through 47 kohm, or driven by an
  SN74LVC1G14DCKR powered from the same rail, with that pull-up still present.

Can TI provide guaranteed maximum currents (a) between data terminals and
(b) from an externally driven data terminal into VCC under these conditions?
Please identify per-pin versus aggregate limits, relevant OE states, and any
required restrictions on supply ramp rate or dwell time. Is there a guaranteed
power-off protection threshold and hysteresis below which all data paths
remain isolated?

We are seeking limits applicable across production variation and the stated
temperature range, rather than a typical simulation or an extrapolation of
Ioff at exactly VCC=0. If these intermediate-supply states are unspecified,
please confirm that limitation and any recommended conditions for guaranteed
isolation.

Thank you.

H2 - TPS389001DSER: SENSE current into an absent or partial VDD supply
Subject: TPS389001DSER - guaranteed SENSE-to-VDD current for VDD below 1.5 V

Hello TI Support,

We are using TPS389001DSER to monitor an independently powered target rail.
SLVSD65A Rev A states that SENSE may operate from 0 to 5.5 V independently of
the supply voltage. We understand this voltage allowance; our question is
about current paths while VDD is absent or below its operating range.

Conditions:
- Ambient temperature: -20 to +60 degC.
- VDD from 0 to 1.5 V, held at intermediate levels or during power ramps;
  include an unpowered high-impedance supply rail as well as VDD held at 0 V.
- SENSE connected to a divider: 169 kohm from an independently powered
  3.0 to 3.47 V target rail, and 100 kohm to GND. Nominal SENSE is therefore
  approximately 1.115 to 1.290 V.
- MR tied to VDD; CT=10 nF. VDD has a 10 kohm bleeder and 9.8 uF nominal
  capacitance. RESET shares a wired-AND net with a second TPS389001DSER
  and is pulled up to VDD through 47 kohm.

What is the guaranteed maximum current transferred from SENSE into VDD in
these conditions? Is an upper clamp or other current path from SENSE to VDD
excluded by design with a guaranteed bound? Please distinguish total SENSE
input current from the portion delivered to the supply rail, and state any
required VDD load/discharge path or supply-sequencing restrictions.

The published 100 nA SENSE-current limit appears under the VDD=1.5 to 5.5 V
table conditions. Can TI confirm whether a guaranteed limit also exists below
1.5 V, including VDD=0? A typical characterization would be useful separately,
but would not replace a guaranteed bound for our qualification.

Thank you.

H3 - TPS70933DBVR: reverse current into an unpowered input/USB VBUS
Subject: TPS70933DBVR - guaranteed OUT-to-IN current below 1.8 V OUT

Hello TI Support,

We are qualifying TPS70933DBVR in a USB interface supply. IN connects to USB
VBUS and EN is intentionally left open. We have reviewed SBVS186H Rev H,
including the reverse-current discussion and the typical 10 nA IN current
at OUT=3 V with IN=EN=0. Our application has EN open and may dwell at lower
OUT voltages, so we would like to confirm the applicable guaranteed limits.

Conditions:
- Ambient temperature: -20 to +60 degC.
- OUT externally biased anywhere from 0 to 3.366 V, including static levels
  and ramps, particularly below 1.8 V.
- IN either held at 0 V or connected to an unpowered, high-impedance VBUS node.
- EN left open. In the actual circuit, IN has 100 nF to GND and a
  200 kohm/75 kohm VBUS divider (275 kohm total path to GND); OUT has
  a 10 kohm bleeder and 9.8 uF nominal capacitance. The high-impedance
  question above also requests the intrinsic device limit; our actual
  VBUS node is therefore not completely unloaded.

What guaranteed maximum OUT-to-IN current applies over this range? Is there
an internal path that can raise an unpowered VBUS node, particularly below
1.8 V OUT? Please specify any required input load, source impedance, sequencing
or ramp conditions. For a high-impedance IN node, please also clarify whether
any bound on the resulting IN voltage is specified.

We need a guaranteed maximum or a guaranteed bounded characteristic with its
validity conditions. Please distinguish this from typical characterization;
we do not assume the typical 10 nA value covers these different conditions.
If no guaranteed limit exists for part of this range, confirmation of that
scope would help us define the remaining design and sample-test requirements.

Thank you.
